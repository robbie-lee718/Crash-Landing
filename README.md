# Crash Landing
You are an astronaut who crashlanded on a random planet. In order to repair your ship you must collect resources that scattered from your ship during the crash, and other resources from the planet.
Your mission is to repair your ship and escape the planet before your oxygen runs out.

## Controls
Walk - wasd
Run - Shift
Jump - Space
Interact - E
Inventory - I

## Objective & How to Play
Collect the required resources to fix your ship before your oxygen runs out.
The resources needed can be shown by walking close to the ship.
Explore the planet and collect resources.
To repair the ship walk back to the ship and interact.
